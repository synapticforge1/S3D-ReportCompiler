Combine reports in the browser

report_core.ts performs no I/O, so the whole bake step runs here unchanged: the maths is pre-rendered, the scripts are stripped and the reports are merged into one printable document. What a browser cannot do is launch a second browser to print, or repack a PDF on disk — so instead of writing a file, this hands the result to the page and lets you print it. The browser is the rendering engine.

Serve this folder over HTTP (npx serve .) rather than opening it from disk, or the fetch below is blocked by the file:// origin policy.
